**YOUTHX** (YouthX is Open Useful Technologies Hub’s Experiments) is an open research collective dedicated to ethical artificial intelligence, open technologies, native programming languages, and practical computing education. We believe that technology must remain free and accessible: if it is open, it is transparent; if it is open, it belongs to everyone.

## Our Mission

We conduct research in artificial intelligence, develop experimental tools and software, and publish our findings openly to contribute to a culture of shared knowledge. Our mission is to push the boundaries of technology while keeping freedom, ethics, and accessibility at the core of everything we do.

## Projects Developed Under YouthX

YouthX serves as the incubator and open-source home for several high-impact technology initiatives:

1. **Hüma Programlama Dili (Hüma Lang):**  
   A high-level programming language designed specifically around Turkish grammar and agglutinative word order, removing the syntax barrier for non-English speakers and speeding up algorithmic learning.

2. **Hilal Browser:**  
   An independent, modern, privacy-focused desktop browser built on Mozilla Gecko with Google Material 3 Expressive UI design and custom productivity features.

3. **NASA Space Apps Challenge (Weather Analysis Platform):**  
   An automated meteorological analysis platform utilizing NASA Earth science datasets (EarthAccess API), Cartopy visualizations, and Google Gemini AI insights ([github.com/YouthX-Research/nasa-hackathon](https://github.com/YouthX-Research/nasa-hackathon)).

4. **Veriden Yapay Zekâya & GençTek Courses:**  
   Comprehensive, hands-on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science training programs delivered to students and young developers across 6 extensive modules.

At the **ICETOL 2026 (International Conference on Educational Technology and Online Learning)**, our paper *"A Turkish Grammar-Aware Approach to Introductory Programming"* was presented internationally, demonstrating the empirical impact of native-language programming syntax.
