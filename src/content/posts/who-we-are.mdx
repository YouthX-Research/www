**YOUTHX** (YouthX is Open Useful Technologies Hub’s Experiments) is an open research collective dedicated to ethical artificial intelligence, open technologies, native programming languages, and practical computing education. We believe that technology must remain free and accessible: if it is open, it is transparent; if it is open, it belongs to everyone.

## Our Mission

We conduct research in artificial intelligence, develop experimental tools and software, and publish our findings openly to contribute to a culture of shared knowledge. Our mission is to push the boundaries of technology while keeping freedom, ethics, and accessibility at the core of everything we do.

## Projects Developed Under YouthX

YouthX serves as the incubator and open-source home for several high-impact technology initiatives:

1. **Hüma Programming Language (Hüma Lang):**  
   A high-level programming language designed specifically around Turkish grammar and agglutinative word order, eliminating the syntax barrier for non-English speakers and accelerating algorithmic understanding.

2. **Hilal Browser:**  
   An independent, modern, privacy-focused desktop browser built on Mozilla Gecko with Google Material 3 Expressive UI design and custom productivity features ([github.com/YouthX-Research/huma-browser](https://github.com/YouthX-Research/huma-browser)).

3. **NASA Space Apps Challenge (Meteorological Analysis Platform):**  
   An automated meteorological analysis platform utilizing NASA Earth science datasets (EarthAccess API), Cartopy geospatial visualizations, and Google Gemini AI insights ([github.com/YouthX-Research/nasa-hackathon](https://github.com/YouthX-Research/nasa-hackathon)).

4. **"From Data to AI" & GençTek AI Courses:**  
   Comprehensive, hands-on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science training programs delivered by Egehan Kahraman across 6 extensive modules covering data wrangling, classical ML algorithms, neural networks, and LLMs.
