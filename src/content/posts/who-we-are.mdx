**YOUTHX** (YouthX is Open Useful Technologies Hub’s Experiments) is an independent research collective dedicated to ethical artificial intelligence, computational linguistics, and the democratization of open computing technologies.

We operate under a foundational conviction: **technology must remain free and accessible**. If software is open, its assumptions are transparent. If research is open, its conclusions can be audited and verified by anyone.

---

## Core Mission & Philosophy

Modern AI systems and programming environments are largely shaped by monolithic corporate interests and Anglo-centric language paradigms. At YouthX, we challenge these structural biases by:

1. **Conducting Empirical Computational Linguistics**: Building rigorous benchmarks to test non-English language understanding, evidentiality, and epistemic modality in large language models.
2. **Eliminating Cognitive & Syntax Barriers**: Developing native programming paradigms and compilers that allow non-English speakers to engage in algorithmic thinking without an English language prerequisite.
3. **Publishing Open Science**: Open weights, open datasets, reproducible methodology scripts, and open-access research papers.

## Active Research Initiatives

### 1. Sezi — Turkish LLM Evidentiality Benchmark
A multi-modal linguistic benchmark testing whether modern frontier language models can accurately distinguish direct observed evidence (`-di`) from reportative or inferential hearsay (`-miş`) across complex Turkish morphological contexts.

### 2. Hüma — Native Turkish Programming Language
An agglutinative, human-centered programming language designed from the ground up according to Turkish grammar and suffix structure, eliminating foreign syntax friction for students and novice programmers.

### 3. Open Evaluation & Ethics Frameworks
Developing diagnostic test benches to measure epistemic honesty, hallucination tendencies, and cultural representation in localized foundational models.
